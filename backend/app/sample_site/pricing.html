How Much Does NexusFlow Cost?

How much does this service cost? NexusFlow plans start at $29 per month for the Starter Tier, $99 per month for the Pro Team Tier, and custom volume pricing for Enterprise Cloud installations. Every plan includes a full-featured 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

1. Starter Tier — $29 per month

Designed for small teams, startups, and individual operators getting started with automation.

  • Up to 10 active automated workflows
  • 15,000 monthly workflow executions
  • 5-minute polling interval with webhook support
  • Access to all standard cloud integrations
  • Email support with 24-hour turnaround

2. Pro Team Tier — $99 per month

Built for growing operations teams that need higher concurrency, sub-second latency, and role-based access control.

  • Up to 50 active automated workflows
  • 100,000 monthly workflow executions
  • Instant real-time webhook routing and event streaming
  • Advanced document parsing with multi-modal AI models
  • Dedicated priority chat support and 99.9% uptime SLA

3. Enterprise Cloud — Custom Annual Pricing

Tailored for high-security enterprises requiring dedicated VPC instances, custom compliance, and unlimited executions.

  • Unlimited workflows and executions
  • Dedicated VPC deployment or on-premises self-hosted agent
  • Custom SSO, SAML, and granular audit log exports
  • Named customer success architect and 99.99% uptime guarantee

Billing & Trial Terms

All plans can be billed monthly or annually with a 20% discount on annual commitments. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time without penalty. Compare our capabilities with alternatives on our NexusFlow comparison page.
