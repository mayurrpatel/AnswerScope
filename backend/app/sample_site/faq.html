How does this product work?

How does this product work? NexusFlow works in three steps: First, connect your business applications and data stores using our library of over 200 certified connectors. Second, design and test your business logic, data mappings, and AI decision nodes in our visual workflow editor. Third, deploy the workflow into production where our resilient event engine executes tasks with automatic retries, concurrency controls, and real-time audit tracing.

What security standards does NexusFlow adhere to?

NexusFlow maintains SOC-2 Type II certification, adheres to GDPR guidelines, and encrypts all customer data using TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest with optional customer-managed KMS keys.
